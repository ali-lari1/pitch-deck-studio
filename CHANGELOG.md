# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-29)
- Images, the right amount: an imagery budget (about 1 slide in 3) with linter warnings for too few or too many image slides.
- New `@side` half-bleed photo directive for any layout (`@side-pos`, `@side-focus`, `@side-width`), `@bg-focus`, and a captioned `gallery` layout.
- Per-image device frames in `screens` (browser + phone together) and a properly rounded phone frame with a camera pill.
- `scripts/find_images.py`: openly licensed photos from Openverse and NASA (no key) or Unsplash/Pexels (with key), with previews and automatic CREDITS.md.
- Examples now use real photography: Unsplash for Dispensa and NASA public-domain imagery for Meridian.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)
- First release: workflow skill, 21 layouts, 11 presets, SVG charts, QA lint and headless overflow check.
- 114-deck library (2022-2026), 23 vertical playbooks, audience variants, fundraising stats, live-sweep protocol.
- Brand extraction from a URL, brand-sheet generator, project scaffolder with 7 story arcs.
- Installers for 14 agents, a Claude/Codex/Grok/Copilot plugin manifest, and a Gemini extension.
