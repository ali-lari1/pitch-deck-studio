# Interview

Goal: get what only the founder knows in **one message**, with no more than 7 questions. Before asking anything,
read the website, the materials and any existing deck, then drop every question already answered.
Prefill your best guess so the founder can just confirm it ("I read this as a seed round for US healthcare VCs.
Correct?"). Confirming takes less effort than typing.

## The question bank (in priority order)

1. **The ask and the reader.** Who is this deck for (angels, pre-seed/seed VC, Series A+, accelerator, corporate/strategic,
   grant, customer/partner), and what do you want from them (amount, instrument, round, pilot)? Name any specific
   investors or programmes.
2. **How it will be used.** Presented live (sparse slides with you talking) or sent ahead and read alone (self-explanatory)?
   Is there a time limit or slide limit? Deadline?
3. **Proof.** What are your strongest real numbers (revenue/ARR, growth, users and retention, pilots, LOIs, waitlist,
   unit economics, technical benchmarks, clinical or lab data)? Include dates and definitions.
4. **Why you, and why now.** What do you know or have that others don't? What changed in the world that makes this possible
   now?
5. **Team.** Founders and key people: one line each on the credential that matters for *this* business. Notable
   advisors or investors already in?
6. **Brand.** Do you have guidelines, a logo, colours or fonts? If not, I'll create a clean minimalist identity. Any
   direction you like or hate (for example "dark and cinematic", "warm and human", "clinical", "bold and minimal")?
7. **Landmines.** What objection do investors raise most, and what do you want to avoid saying?

## Optional follow-ups (only if the answers leave gaps that block the story)
- Competitors you are compared to, and the honest reason you win.
- Business model specifics (pricing, ACV, take rate, margins) and use of funds.
- Customer quotes you can use (with permission), and logos you are allowed to show.
- Geography or regulatory context (FDA, FCA, CE, ITAR, data residency...).

## Delegation mode
If the founder says "just do it", or doesn't answer within the session, proceed. Make conservative assumptions and
write them at the top of `brief.md` under **Assumptions to confirm**. Use placeholders for every missing number.
Never fill a gap with a plausible-sounding invented figure.

## Reading signals instead of asking
| Signal | Inference |
|---|---|
| "Join waitlist", no pricing page | pre-revenue, pre-seed/seed; lead with insight, team and early demand |
| Pricing page, customer logos, case studies | revenue stage; lead with traction |
| Careers page with 20+ roles | Series A+ |
| Docs site, GitHub link, API reference | developer audience; show code and product, cite stars and usage |
| Publications, clinical-trial IDs, patents | science-led deck; credibility and data first |
| Government or defense customers | sober mission tone; show contract vehicles and pilots |
| Non-English site / regional domain | consider regional investors and language; see `audiences.md` |
