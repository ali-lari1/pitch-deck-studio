## 3. Market climate (Q2-Q3 2026)
- Concentration: rounds of $100M+ took 87.5% of US venture dollars in H1 2026; flat/down rounds only 13.1% of deals, lowest since 2022; AI median step-up 2.2x vs 1.6x non-AI ([PitchBook via TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/pitchbook-q2-2026-megadeals-87-percent-us-venture-vintage-discount)).
- AI share of US deal value: 82.7% YTD through Q3 2026, but 65.9% in Q3 alone; Q3 deal value ~$98.4B (-~40% QoQ), YTD $515.8B ([SiliconANGLE on PitchBook-NVCA Q3 2026, 8 Oct 2026](https://siliconangle.com/?p=849755); secondary).
- CVC involvement in a record 82.6% of US deal value in H1 2026, driven by late-stage AI ([Global Venturing citing PitchBook](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/overview/corporates-broaden-the-venture-toolkit/?format=md)).
- Acquisitions: $375.4B in value, a decade high; median step-up 1.9x (TNW above). Exits are open: this changes late-seed story ("who buys us") more than it changes the A.
- Seed investors now quote an ARR bar of $2-3M (sometimes $4M) at Series A "in the AI era" ([Crunchbase quoting Uncork, May 2026, via Waveup](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/)); one summary says some A investors want $5-10M ([Carta, via search summary](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/)). Pre-2024 anchor: Series A ARR mostly $1-5M, >half growing >2x ([Point Nine via SaaStr, 2023](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/), pre-2024).

## 4. Universal efficiency benchmarks (cross-vertical)
- Burn multiple: ~2x reasonable early, >=3x suggests PMF problem; 1-1.5x good at <$25M ARR (David Sacks 2020; Point Nine 2023; pre-2024) via Waveup Series A page.
- Growth: 2-3x YoY at $1-2M ARR, ~2x at $5-10M (Point Nine/SaaStr Sept 2024); median growth at $1-5M ARR 50%, top quartile 100%; AI-native median 110% vs B2B SaaS 40% (High Alpha through Q2 2025).
- NRR: median 104%, top quartile 110% at $1-5M ARR (High Alpha, Q2 2025); Bessemer tiers 100/110/120% (2023, pre-2024).
- Dilution: ~18% priced seed (Carta 2026); founders retain ~56% after seed (Carta 2021-25 rounds).
