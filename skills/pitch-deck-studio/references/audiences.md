# Audience variants (as of 2026-10-29)

Legend: **[S]** = from 2026 live sweep (linked); **[B]** = background knowledge, verify. The skill should treat each variant as a transform on the vertical playbook: slide order, emphasis, length, tone, and the "ask".

## Angels
- Facts: median individual check ~$25-50K, range $10K-$2M; angels rarely lead and usually fill a round after a lead or anchor; most do not reserve for follow-on, so plan new investors at A ([mean.ceo angel stats 2026](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=10271), [Ellty angel guide](https://www.ellty.com/blog/angel-investors); secondary). A $500K pre-seed may need 10-20 angels (same sources).
- Deck: 8-10 slides, team-first. Open with founder-market fit and one proof point (waitlist, LOIs, shipped demo). Replace detailed financial model with use of funds and 18-month milestones. Plain-language problem; no jargon. State SAFE cap, round size, committed amount, and what the check unlocks.
- Cut: TAM theatre, 5-year projections, cap-table detail. Add: "why me" story, who has already committed, ask for intros/customers as well as cash.
- Tone: personal, warm, concise. Often read on mobile; one idea per slide, big type.

## Pre-seed / seed VCs
- Facts: seed median $3.0M all sectors (PitchBook-NVCA H1 2026), $4.1M software at $24.3M post (Carta, to Jul 2026); pre-seed avg instrument $276K, SAFE 93% ([Carta Q2 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-q2-2026/)). See `fundraising-stats.md`.
- Deck: 10-12 slides. Order: one-liner/problem -> product (screens/demo) -> why now -> traction or leading indicators -> market (bottom-up) -> business model -> team -> ask. Insight/wedge and "why you, why now" outrank financials.
- Promote: insight, wedge-to-expansion map, earliest retention/usage cohort, design-partner list. Demote: competition grid (keep one honest 2x2), detailed financials (one slide: burn, runway, 18-24 month milestones).
- Add vertical-specific slide per playbooks (e.g. regulatory path in health, pilot pipeline in hardware/defense).
- Tone: conviction + specificity; show the product by slide 3.

## Series A+ VCs
- Facts: median software Series A $14.4M at $80M post (Carta, 6 months to Jul 2026); US all-sector pre-money $62-64M (PitchBook 2026); AI vs non-AI pre-money $78M vs $42M. ARR bar reported $2-3M+ with 100%+ growth for top funds ([Waveup Series A benchmarks](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/), compiled; vendor blog). Burn multiple <2x, NRR 110%+ common asks.
- Deck: 14-18 slides plus appendix. Metrics move front: ARR/growth chart by slide 3-4, cohort retention, NRR, CAC payback, burn multiple, pipeline coverage, sales-efficiency/GTM repeatability, hiring plan, 24-month plan and what Series B requires. Team slide shows who you'll hire.
- Promote: unit economics, cohort curves, GTM playbook, competitive win/loss, customer concentration. Demote: vision slide (shorter), origin story.
- Prepare data-room-ready appendix (cohort tables, pipeline, pricing history). Tone: numeric, calm, no adjectives.

## Accelerators (YC, Techstars, Antler, EF, etc.)
- Terms: YC standard deal $500K = $125K for 7% post-money SAFE + $375K uncapped MFN SAFE ([RoundFunded summary 2026](https://www.roundfunded.com/blogs/yc-standard-deal-equity-explained-2026)); Techstars reported $220K = $20K for 5% + $200K uncapped MFN post-money SAFE ([Causo guide, 2026](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-techstars-2026); verify on techstars.com). YC Winter 2027 applications reported open Oct 16 - Nov 2 2026 ([mean.ceo newsletter](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11471)); YC Demo Day deck rules are partly [B].
- Application is not a deck: 1-min founder video, short answers, traction chart. If a deck is requested: 6-8 slides, 1 idea each; founders' unfair insight, growth rate in weeks, what you will do in 3 months.
- Demo-day deck (for investors): 1-2 min pitch; one metric chart (weekly growth), one-liner, ask. Strip jargon; partners read hundreds.
- Promote: speed of learning, weekly growth, founders' technical depth. Cut: long TAM, polished financial model.

## Corporate VC / strategics
- Facts: CVCs participated in a record 82.6% of US deal value in H1 2026 (late-stage AI, PitchBook via [Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/overview/corporates-broaden-the-venture-toolkit/?format=md)); on deal count CVC involvement is >25% and steady ([Affinity](https://www.affinity.co/blog/corporate-venture-capital-trends)); CVCs close a higher share of AI deals (63% vs 49% for VCs, Affinity). Aranca/PitchBook: investors favor category leaders and strategically aligned platforms ([Aranca Q2 2026](https://www.aranca.com/assets/docs/Quarterly_Spotlight_CVC_Deal_Analysis_Q2_2026.pdf)).
- Deck: add "strategic fit" slide naming the corporate's business unit, product, or customer overlap; "commercial agreement" slide (pilot, reseller, integration, supply); IP/standards and exclusivity risk (what they get, what they do not). Address conflicts: will the strategic block other buyers/partners, info rights, ROFR.
- Promote: integration, joint GTM, reference customers inside corporate's industry. Demote: pure financial returns narrative (keep one slide).
- Tone: partnership; speak to the sponsor's internal metric (cost-out, revenue, supply security). Expect long timelines; include pilot milestones with dates.

## Grants / non-dilutive
- US: SBIR/STTR lapsed Oct 1 2025 for ~6 months; reauthorized Apr 13 2026 through Sept 30 2031; DoW resumed solicitations, others slower; new "strategic breakthrough" Phase II class; FY2027 proposal caps; stricter foreign-ownership screening ([Crowell alert](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/sbirsttr-programs-reauthorized-after-six-month-lapse)). Warning: a new VC round can create SBIR affiliation problems (legal analysis in sweep; check with counsel).
- EU: EIC Accelerator grants up to EUR 2.5M (70% of eligible cost) for TRL 6-8 plus equity EUR 1-10M; step 1 is short form + 10-slide deck + 3-min video; 2026 full-proposal cut-offs include 4 Nov ([EIC news Jun 2026](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/news/38-start-ups-and-smes-secure-eic-support-latest-round-eic-accelerator-2026-06-15_en); details via [Intelectium](https://intelectium.com/en/post/eic-accelerator-2026-la-financiacion-europea-que-impulsa-a-las-startups-deep-tech-con-potencial-de-liderar-mercados-globales), secondary; verify call text).
- Deck becomes a proposal: technical innovation and TRL, work plan with milestones/deliverables, risk register, IP/FTO, impact (jobs, emissions, market), consortium/partners, budget by work package. Investors' "why now/TAM" shrinks; reviewers' criteria (excellence, impact, implementation) drive order. Tone: formal, measured, evidence-referenced, every claim traceable.

## Sovereign / regional funds (MENA/GCC)
- Facts: MENA H1 2026 funding $1.35B (-22% YoY), 214 deals, lowest half-year since 2022; regional investors supplied ~81% of capital; AI took ~50% ($678M) of funding, 93% of it in UAE; Saudi fintech funding fell 41% ([MAGNiTT H1 2026](https://www.magnitt.com/research/mena-venture-capital-the-h1-2026-review-51047), [AI note](https://www.magnitt.com/news/ai-companies-captured-half-of-mena-s-venture-funding-in-h1-2026-from-just-a-quarter-of-all-deals-54044)). Wamda reports different totals ($1.7B); definitions differ. 17 MENA venture funds targeting $1.65B since 2024 had deployed $197M (MAGNiTT).
- Expect a local-presence ask: PIF reportedly pushing capital toward in-kingdom operations, so foreign startups may need a Saudi office ([Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/saudi-investment-europe-startups-pif)); QIA expects senior presence in Qatar for fund managers. Sovereign-backed vehicles (Mubadala, Jada, Lunate in LPs for [Shorooq](https://www.hortidaily.com/article/9674544/uae-headquartered-shorooq-aims-to-be-middle-east-s-blackstone/)). Example: QIA co-led Gatik's $200M Series D (Aug 2026, [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/gatik-secures-funding-qia-493195/)).
- Deck: add "Why the Gulf / local impact" slide: local hiring, data-residency, regional HQ, offtake/customers in KSA/UAE/Qatar, alignment with national programs (Vision 2030, AI strategies [B]); localization plan; government/enterprise anchor customers; bilingual (Arabic/English) one-pager. Promote: strategic/industrial fit, scale ambition, credentialed advisors/co-investors. Tone: formal, relationship-led, institutional; expect multiple meetings and a longer cycle. Check Islamic-finance/Shariah screening where relevant [B].

## Customer / partner decks
- Not an investor deck. Structure: their problem (in their language) -> outcome with number -> how it works (one diagram) -> proof (case study, pilot results, logos) -> integration/security/compliance -> pricing/ROI -> rollout plan and timeline -> next step. 8-12 slides, tailored per account.
- Cut: round, valuation, TAM, competitor bashing. Add: security/compliance badges (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001), SLAs, references, implementation effort, total cost of ownership, procurement path.
- Tone: operational and specific; buyer champion should be able to forward it internally unchanged. Storydoc-style personalization ("what we heard from you") reportedly lifts engagement +31% (vendor stat, unverified; see `fundraising-stats.md`).
