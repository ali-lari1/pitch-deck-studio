# Gaming

Legend: linked = 2026 sweep; unlinked = background, verify; **[pre-2024]** = older benchmark.

## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: gaming specialists (BITKRAFT, Makers Fund, Konvoy, Griffin Gaming Partners, Transcend, Play Ventures, Hiro Capital, Lightspeed Games, a16z Games, Courtside, Sisu, Bitkraft); publishers (Tencent, Krafton, Netease, Nexon); platform funds (Roblox, Unity ecosystem); web3 gaming funds (Konvoy, Animoca); angels from studios.
- Evidence: Konvoy Fund III $150M (pre-seed to A; AUM ~$258-270M), BITKRAFT ~$1.05B AUM seed-B $3-30M+ (19 deals 2025), Makers Fund ~$960M AUM (6 deals) ([Peony gaming investors](https://peony.ink/blog/top-gaming-investors), [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/konvoy-ventures-launches-150m-investment-fund/); blog listings, verify).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Retention (D1/D7/D30) vs genre benchmark in soft launch.
2. Monetization and unit economics: ARPDAU, LTV vs UA cost (CPI), payback window.
3. Team's hit history and shipping record; studio capacity.
4. IP/genre fit and differentiation (UGC, live-ops, platform tailwinds); platform risk (app stores, Roblox).
5. Cost to reach content-complete and marketing budget.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 12-16.
- Promote: **gameplay video/trailer (slide 2)**, **retention + monetization data from soft launch**, **team track record**.
- Add: **content roadmap and live-ops plan**, **UA/marketing plan with CPI**, **platform strategy**, **IP/licensing**.
- Demote: TAM of "global gamers"; long narrative lore.

## 5. Proof devices
Playable build link/video; soft-launch cohort table vs GameAnalytics medians; Steam wishlist counts (PC) and wishlist-to-sale conversion; creator/streamer reach; prior hit rankings; playtest quotes.

## 6. Design and tone
Visual and energetic: game art, trailers, GIFs, strong key art; but disciplined data slide(s). Show gameplay in first 3 slides. Studio-voice, not marketing-speak.

## 7. Red flags
D1 under genre median; no soft-launch data; UA-dependent growth with CPI > payback; first-time team with ambitious scope; platform dependency; cost overrun on content; web3 "play-to-earn" mechanics without gameplay.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Funding stabilizing near $9.8B (H1 2026) with growth-stage +22% (Konvoy); Q1 2026 seed/A surge (conflicting figures).
- GameAnalytics reports retention weak at the median; investors expect genre-appropriate benchmarks and soft-launch evidence.
- Specialist funds remain small relative to the market (BITKRAFT ~$1B, Makers ~$1B AUM).
