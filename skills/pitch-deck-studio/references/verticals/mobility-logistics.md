## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: mobility/logistics specialists (Fifth Wall for freight real estate, Maersk Growth, DHL/UPS/FedEx ventures, Toyota Ventures, Eclipse, Trucks VC, Lemnis, Dynamo, Wing, Initialized supply-chain angels); autonomy investors (Nvidia, Koch Disruptive, Qualcomm); sovereign funds (QIA); growth/hedge funds (Millennium, ARK); strategic OEMs and shippers.
- Evidence: Gatik $200M Series D (Aug 25 2026) led by QIA and Koch Disruptive Technologies, with Millennium, ARK, Intact; >$600M contracted revenue, 85,000 driverless orders, target >100 driverless trucks by end-2026 ([DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/gatik-secures-funding-qia-493195/), [Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/gatik-brings-in-200m-to-continue-expanding-autonomous-trucking-operations/)); KargoBot (China) Series B Mar 2026, $285.9M total ([Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/kargobot)); notable freight names Aurora, Flexport, Sennder, project44 ([Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/guide/haulage-logistics-startups-reshaping-freight/)).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Customer contracts and recurring volume (contracted revenue, repeat shippers) not pilots.
2. Unit economics per mile/shipment/stop; asset-light vs asset-heavy; cost per delivery vs incumbent.
3. Safety/regulatory approval for autonomy; operating design domain; permits by state/country.
4. Freight-cycle and fuel/rate exposure; concentration among shippers.
5. Capital efficiency: cost to scale fleet; financing via debt/leasing.

- Promote: **shipper contracts and repeat volume**, **unit economics per mile/stop**, **safety and regulatory status**, **operations dashboard**.

## 5. Proof devices
Driverless mile counts and safety record; shipper logos and volumes; live fleet dashboard; cost-per-mile vs incumbent chart; regulator permits; ride-along video.

## 6. Design and tone
Operational and industrial: maps, lanes, dashboards, vehicle photography; precise ops vocabulary; safety-first.

## 7. Red flags
Pilots perpetually "in testing"; unit economics requiring safety driver forever; single shipper; regulatory dependence; asset-heavy without financing; freight cyclicality ignored.
