## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Physics/engineering credibility: is the core claim real, reproducible, and independently validated?
2. Technical roadmap and de-risking: TRL ladder, key milestones (qubit fidelity, yield, throughput), time/cost to each.
3. Commercial wedge before the grand vision: near-term product sold to someone (metrology, simulation, inspection) rather than waiting for the full machine.
4. IP and moat: patents, trade secrets, fab/lab access, supplier concentration.
5. Capital plan and non-dilutive stack; strategic partner/customer interest (foundry, hyperscaler, defense).

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 14-20.
- Promote: **technical differentiator with data**, **roadmap with milestone gates and cost**, **IP/moat**, **commercial wedge**.
- Add: **TRL ladder and manufacturability**, **supply chain (fab, materials, helium/dilution fridges)**, **non-dilutive funding plan**, **team scientific pedigree & advisors**.
- Demote: market-size theatre; sci-fi vision; hype comparisons.

## 5. Proof devices
Measured results vs state-of-the-art plot (with dates and error bars); peer-reviewed papers/preprints; third-party validation (national lab, foundry); wafer/chip photos; customer LOIs from foundries/primes; patents list; roadmap with go/no-go gates.

## 6. Design and tone
Scientific and sober: data-dense charts, labeled axes, schematics, lab/fab photography; minimal gloss; humility about timelines. Distinguish demonstrated vs projected clearly.

## 7. Red flags
Roadmap hand-waves from physics demo to product; no near-term revenue wedge; untested at manufacturing yield; hype comparisons (quantum advantage claims); dependence on single lab/fab; IP from university unlicensed; talent concentration; capital need outruns milestones.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Quantum VC >$4B in 2026 YTD (FT/PitchBook, secondhand); Europe's deeptech EUR 2.0B across 103 rounds mid-2026.
- Hybrid financing (grants + equity) normal: Chips Act/EIC covers a large share (QuantumDiamonds EUR 76M grants).
- Defense and sovereign interest (In-Q-Tel, Intel) is a pull for quantum/semis (see defense-space).
