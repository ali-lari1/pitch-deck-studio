## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: agrifoodtech specialists (AgFunder, Anterra, Rabo Food & Agri Innovation Fund, Syngenta Ventures, Cultivian, S2G, Astanor, Better Food Ventures, Synthesis Capital, Five Seasons, Mirova, Flagship for biologicals); strategics (Bayer, Corteva, Nestle, Cargill, Ecolab, Mars, PepsiCo); climate-VCs for ag-emissions; development/blended finance and government (USDA, EU).
- Evidence: Techstars Future of Food (powered by Ecolab) is an active accelerator ([listing](https://www.heysuccess.com/opportunity/Techstars-Future-of-Food-Powered-by-Ecolab-57294)); deeptech and biologicals draw capital as a hedge against fertilizer volatility ([Fertilizer Daily / Global Ag Investing](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260701-agtech-investment-five-growth-themes-2026/md/)). Specific 2026 lead investors by name were not surfaced; verify in live sweep (AgFunder report).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Unit economics at commercial scale (cost per kg / per acre vs incumbent); "tangible science, real unit economics, clear path to revenue" ([AgFunder 2026](https://agfunder.com/research/agfunder-global-agrifoodtech-investment-report-2026/)).
2. Farmer/grower ROI and adoption: payback within one season; distribution through retailers/co-ops.
3. Regulatory approvals (EPA, FDA, USDA, EU novel food), organic/biological efficacy trials across seasons.
4. Capital intensity (plants, bioreactors) and financing (project debt, offtake).
5. Seasonality and weather risk; concentration among a few buyers.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 13-17.
- Promote: **grower/consumer ROI**, **field trial data**, **unit cost curve**, **regulatory approvals and timeline**, **distribution channel**.
- Add: **seasonality & working capital**, **offtake/retail partners**, **capex and financing plan**, **IP/biology**.
- Demote: sustainability narrative without economics; generic population-growth TAM.

## 5. Proof devices
Multi-season yield or efficacy charts (with controls); farmer testimonials and acreage under contract; retailer shelf data; regulatory approval letters; cost-of-goods teardown; pilot plant photos.

## 6. Design and tone
Grounded and practical: field/plant photography, clear trial charts, farmer vocabulary; scientific rigor for biotech inputs; avoid green-wash. Alt-protein/food brand: add consumer-facing visuals and taste/price proof.

## 7. Red flags
Unproven farmer ROI; single-season trial; capex-heavy plants without offtake; consumer food brand with high CAC; regulatory timeline ignored; commodity price exposure; reliance on subsidies.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Funding flat at $16.2B in 2025; investors selective (AgFunder).
- Biologicals and nutrient-efficiency technologies favored as fertilizer hedges; deeptech dominates dollars (Global Ag Investing, Fertilizer Daily).
- US agtech VC at slowest pace in series H1 2026 (PitchBook-NVCA via Farmonaut); food delivery/eGrocery collapsing.
