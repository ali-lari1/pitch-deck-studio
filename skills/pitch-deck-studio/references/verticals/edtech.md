# Edtech

Legend: linked = 2026 sweep; unlinked = background, verify; **[pre-2024]** = older benchmark.

## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: education specialists (Reach Capital, Learn Capital, Owl Ventures, GSV, Emerge Education, Rethink Education, Brighteye, Gradient, Tiger Global legacy); platforms and AI funds for AI tutoring; corporate (Microsoft, Google, Pearson); foundations (Gates, Chan Zuckerberg, Walton) for non-dilutive and efficacy; workforce-learning investors.
- Evidence: Reach Capital ($215M Fund IV in 2023, 130+ education startups, seed $500K-5M checks) and Learn Capital (Coursera, Udemy, Photomath) ([Ellty](https://www.ellty.com/blog/elearning-investors), [Peony](https://peony.ink/blog/top-edtech-investors); vendor listings); Subject and Gizmo raised significant K-12 AI rounds ([HolonIQ note](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/1b-in-edtech-venture-capital-to-date-funding-falls-short-of-last-years-midpoint-asia-mena-buck-the-trend)).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Who buys and how: district/school procurement, parents, employers, learners; sales cycle and budget cycle.
2. Learning efficacy: evidence of outcomes (RCT, ESSA tier, pass rates), not engagement.
3. Retention/engagement and distribution (consumer learning) or contract renewal rates (institutional).
4. Differentiation vs AI tutors from labs and Khan/Duolingo-type incumbents.
5. Compliance and safety (FERPA, COPPA, GDPR-K), content licensing.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 11-14.
- Promote: **efficacy/outcomes evidence**, **buyer and procurement path**, **engagement/retention**, **AI tutor differentiation**.
- Add: **compliance (FERPA/COPPA)**, **content and IP**, **pilot-to-contract conversion**, **teacher/user workflow**.
- Demote: TAM of global education spending; vanity downloads.

## 5. Proof devices
Before/after learning gains with n and effect size; district testimonials and renewals; classroom video; teacher time saved; pilot conversion table; third-party efficacy report; usage heatmaps.

## 6. Design and tone
Warm, trustworthy, human: real classrooms/learners (consented), accessible type, plain language; scientific credibility on efficacy; avoid hype about "replacing teachers".

## 7. Red flags
Engagement without learning evidence; long sales cycles with no pilot conversion; reliance on one district; student data compliance gaps; AI tutor commoditized; consumer CAC > LTV; seasonal budget misfit.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Cautious market: edtech VC ~$1B in H1 2026 (-26% YoY per HolonIQ summary); AI tutoring and workforce learning draw capital.
- AI-enabled personalization gains traction in K-12 (HolonIQ note); Asia and MENA buck the downtrend (HolonIQ headline).
