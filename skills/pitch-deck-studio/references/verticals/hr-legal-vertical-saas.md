Another source cites a later Harvey round at $15.5B (unverified).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Workflow depth and system-of-record status in the vertical (embedding in how work is done and billed).
2. Pricing shift: seats vs outcomes/usage as AI replaces labor; willingness to pay from labor budgets, not software budgets.
3. Accuracy, auditability, and liability (legal/HR: hallucination risk, bias, compliance with employment/AI laws).
4. Customer concentration and segment (BigLaw vs SMB; enterprise HR vs SMB).
5. Data access and moats (proprietary documents/benchmarks), integrations (iManage, HRIS, payroll).

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 11-14.
- Promote: **workflow/product demo in a real matter or hiring flow**, **ROI per customer (hours/$ saved)**, **pricing model**, **customer logos in the vertical**.
- Add: **accuracy & audit trail**, **compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, privilege/confidentiality, EEOC/AI hiring laws)**, **integration map**, **expansion path to adjacent workflows**.
- Demote: horizontal AI claims; large TAM without segment depth.

## 5. Proof devices
Time-and-motion before/after; accuracy benchmarks vs human; named firm/company testimonials; usage per seat; security attestations; expansion logo journey (pilot -> department -> firm-wide); short sample deliverable (redline, shortlist).

## 6. Design and tone
Professional, trust-oriented: conservative palette, clean UI, domain vocabulary (legal/HR), emphasis on auditability. Understated confidence; avoid "replace lawyers/recruiters" rhetoric.

## 7. Red flags
Wrapper features that incumbents can bundle (Thomson Reuters, Workday); hallucination/liability events; low seat expansion; pricing under pressure from model costs; customer concentration in few big firms; services-heavy delivery.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Legal AI is a winner-take-most market: three companies took ~63% of $2.3B in Q1 2026 (Artificial Lawyer); Legora tripled valuation in five months.
- Vertical AI rounds priced at premium multiples; Harvey and Legora arms race for BigLaw.
