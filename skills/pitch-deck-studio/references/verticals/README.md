emphasis, proof devices, design and tone, red flags, market context, and **live-sweep queries** to refresh it at run time.
Inside the files, linked claims came from the 2026 research sweep, while unlinked investor names and `[B]` items are background
knowledge. Verify them in the live sweep before putting them on a slide. `[pre-2024]` marks older benchmarks.

## Hybrids
Most startups straddle two verticals. Load both files. The **buyer's** vertical decides proof, tone and design (a hospital
buyer expects clinical rigour even from an AI company), and the **technology** vertical decides the moat and risk questions.

## Verticals not listed
Use the closest file plus a live sweep built from its query templates, and document the choice in `brief.md`.
