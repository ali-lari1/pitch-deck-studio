## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: marketplace specialists (Lightspeed, Accel, a16z, Sequoia, Benchmark, Index, Balderton, Atomico, Cherry, Version One, Forerunner, 645 Ventures, Slow Ventures); YC; vertical B2B marketplace funds; growth (General Atlantic, Insight) for later.
- Evidence: Accel, a16z, Sequoia, Benchmark appear in marketplace guides ([Waveup marketplace VCs](https://waveup.com/blog/top-20-marketplace-venture-capital-firms/), vendor list). Marketplace startups raised ~$4.8B in 2025, +27% vs 2024 (same guide; verify).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Liquidity: supply/demand balance, fill rate, match rate, time to match.
2. Repeat/retention: cohort repeat rate and GMV retention (the engine of marketplace value).
3. Take rate and its trajectory; multi-tenanting and disintermediation (leakage).
4. Supply acquisition cost and quality; cold-start strategy (single-player tool, geo density).
5. Unit economics at the transaction level: contribution margin after incentives, payments, support.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 11-13.
- Promote: **liquidity metrics and cohort repeat**, **supply-side acquisition engine**, **take rate/unit economics**.
- Add: **cold-start and density plan**, **disintermediation defense**, **trust & safety / payments flow**.
- Demote: broad TAM; logo walls without GMV.

## 5. Proof devices
GMV cohort chart (by acquisition month), repeat purchase curve, fill-rate gauge, supply waitlist, take-rate waterfall, supply/demand screenshots, case study of a "power seller".

## 6. Design and tone
Two-sided clarity: show both sides' value in parallel visuals; clean data charts; community/brand feel for consumer-facing, professional for B2B.

## 7. Red flags
Subsidized GMV; low repeat; disintermediation; take rate cut to compete; supply concentrated in a few sellers; high fraud/refunds; unit economics rely on perpetual incentives.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Marketplaces raised ~$4.8B in 2025 (+27%) per vendor guide; fewer, larger seed rounds in Q1 2026 ([Causo](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/raising-seed-marketplace-startup-2026)).
- AI-native vertical marketplaces (services plus software) is the hot reframe [B].
- Food delivery and eGrocery marketplaces declining in funding (AgFunder: largest 2025 mega-deal 80% below 2021 peak, [Global Ag Investing](https://globalaginvesting.com/lessons-from-the-2026-global-agrifoodtech-report/)).
