## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. FDA pathway and timeline (510(k)/De Novo/PMA), predicate or clinical-trial burden; EU MDR notified-body timelines.
2. Clinical evidence plan and endpoint that drives adoption; trial cost and timing.
3. Reimbursement: CPT/DRG/NTAP path, who pays, coding strategy; hospital value-analysis committee (VAC) fit.
4. Cost of goods, manufacturing scale-up, QMS (ISO 13485).
5. Adoption: physician champion, learning curve, procedure workflow, exit via strategics.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 14-20.
- Promote: **regulatory & reimbursement path** with timeline and cost, clinical evidence plan, physician champions, unit cost / COGS curve.
- Add: **IP**, **manufacturing & QMS**, **risk (FMEA) summary**, **hospital procurement path**.
- Demote: consumer-style growth; vague "AI" language without regulatory stance.

## 5. Proof devices
Bench and animal data tables; first-in-human images/videos (with consent); KOL surgeon quotes; FDA pre-sub letters/breakthrough designation; reimbursement code evidence; prototype in hand, cost teardown.

## 6. Design and tone
Clinical credibility: clean medical imagery, procedure diagrams, restrained palette, precise numbers (n, endpoints, CI). Calm, safety-first language.

## 7. Red flags
No predicate or clinical strategy; reimbursement unknown; underpriced trial budget; manufacturing at prototype stage; single-physician dependence; burn on pivotal trial without runway; IP design-around risk; 510(k) assumed when PMA likely.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- $8B venture across 278 rounds through Q3 2026 vs $2.5B in Q1 alone: capital skewed to later-stage assets ([JPM Q3](https://www.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpmorgan/documents/cb/insights/outlook/jpm-medtech-deck-q3-2026.pdf)).
- Seed/A annualized -17% YoY; investors want clinical/regulatory de-risking ([JPM Q2](https://www.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpmorgan/documents/cb/insights/outlook/jpm-medtech-deck-q2-2026-2.pdf), [Bioxconomy](https://www.bioxconomy.com/partnering/medtech-sector-stabilizes-as-investors-favor-late-stage-deals-and-assets)).
