## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Path to a funded program: SBIR Phase II->III, OTA/prototype, program of record; who in the government will buy (named program office, requirement).
2. Valley-of-death plan: only 16% of DoD SBIR firms won Phase III in a decade ([DIB 2023](https://innovation.defense.gov/Portals/63/DIB_Terraforming%20the%20Valley%20of%20Death_230717_1.pdf)); <1% of Phase I awardees reach a program of record ([J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/investing/investment-trends/defense-tech-innovation-and-the-role-of-startups)).
3. Dual-use revenue: commercial sales offset long government timelines; software firms with commercial revenue ahead of hardware ([JD Supra / trade piece](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/scaling-defense-tech-in-2026-and-beyond-3157614/), opinion).
4. Technical readiness (TRL) and manufacturability at rate; export controls (ITAR/EAR), clearances, supply chain (no adversary parts).
5. Team: credible ex-military/primes; ability to navigate acquisition.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 12-18.
- Promote: **mission need / requirement**, **government customer & contract vehicle path**, **TRL and test results**, **dual-use commercial market**, **manufacturing at rate**.
- Add: **compliance (ITAR/export/cyber CMMC)**, **SBIR affiliation note for cap table** (a new venture round can create affiliation/ineligibility issues), **supply chain provenance**.
- Demote: consumer-style growth/virality; buzzword-heavy "disruption".

## 5. Proof devices
Test-range or field-exercise video; signed CRADA/OTA/SBIR awards; letters of support from program offices or operators; TRL ladder with dated evidence; CONOPS one-pager; cost-per-effect vs legacy system; production readiness photos.

## 6. Design and tone
Sober, mission-first, dark/neutral palette, precise terminology, operator-centric. Avoid hype and weapon-glorifying imagery; specify classification handling (public vs CUI). Space: engineering plus mission clarity.

## 7. Red flags
No named buyer; reliance on a single SBIR; "dual-use" with no commercial customers; foreign-ownership or supply-chain exposure; 24-month procurement assumed to close in 6; pricing far above incumbents without capability delta; cap table issues with SBIR affiliation.

## 8. 2025-26 context
