# E-commerce / D2C / CPG

Legend: linked = 2026 sweep; unlinked = background, verify; **[pre-2024]** = older benchmark.

## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: consumer-brand specialists (Forerunner, Imaginary, Collaborative Fund, Burgeon, Thrive, Lerer Hippeau, Maveron, 1984, Springdale, Slow, Brand Foundry, Nosh Fund); CPG strategics (Unilever, PepsiCo, General Mills Bold Ventures); growth/revenue-based lenders (Clearco-type, Wayflyer, Shopify Capital); celebrity/angels; retail-media aware funds; family offices.
- Evidence: the sweep found no reliable 2026 D2C investor ranking; a Peony post notes DTC funding "fell 97%" from peak (vendor; verify) ([Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/top-b2b-marketplace-investors)). Forerunner reviews cohort retention closely (anecdote, same source family). Capital increasingly comes as debt/revenue-based finance; verify in live sweep.

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Contribution margin after fulfillment, returns, marketing; "contribution margin and payback now drive valuation conversations more than LTV:CAC" ([Tenten 2026](https://tenten.co/shopify/ecommerce-funding-2026-what-investors-want-d2c/)).
2. Cohort repeat purchase: "show me month-12 repeat rate per cohort" (same source).
3. Gross margin and pricing power vs supply-chain/tariff inflation.
4. Channel mix: paid social dependence vs retention (email/SMS) vs retail/wholesale distribution; retail velocity (units/store/week) for CPG.
5. Brand differentiation and category tailwind; inventory risk.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 10-13.
- Promote: **unit economics (per order contribution waterfall)**, **cohort repeat chart**, **brand/product visuals**, **channel mix**.
- Add: **supply chain & tariff exposure**, **retail velocity (for CPG)**, **inventory & working capital**, **brand moat/community**.
- Demote: TAM by population; influencer vanity metrics.

## 5. Proof devices
Per-order P&L waterfall; cohort repeat curve; retail sell-through; NPS/reviews; creative/ad performance table; product in hand/packaging shots; press and retailer letters.

## 6. Design and tone
Brand-forward and visual: strong photography, packaging, lifestyle imagery; tight story; but investors expect a rigorous unit economics page. Aspirational yet numerate.

## 7. Red flags
Growth that depends on rising paid CAC; contribution margin negative after returns; discount-driven repeat; heavy inventory; single-channel (Meta/Amazon); tariff exposure with no plan; low gross margin.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Capital scarce for classic D2C; investors want contribution margin, payback, and month-12 repeat (Tenten, Eightx, 2026 vendor blogs).
- Supply-chain inflation squeezes low-margin brands (Tenten); tariff pass-through data not found in sweep; add live search.
- Retail media/distribution strategy and profitability preferred over growth.
