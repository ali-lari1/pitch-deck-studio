## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Does it work in the field (reliability, success rate, MTBF) vs demo-ware?
2. Unit economics: BOM -> COGS -> gross margin glide; yield, DFM plan, supplier concentration ([vendor guide 2026](https://www.vcsheet.com/sheet/investors-focused-on-hardware)).
3. Customer pull: paid pilots, purchase orders, RaaS contracts, pilot-to-fleet conversion.
4. Capital intensity and runway to manufacturing scale; tooling and MOQ.
5. Defensibility: data flywheel/foundation-model for robotics, IP, vs Chinese cost competition.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 12-16.
- Promote: **live demo video**, **unit-economics/BOM-to-COGS curve**, **pilot pipeline with conversion**, **manufacturing plan**.
- Add: **reliability data (MTBF, success rate)**, **supply-chain map and second sources**, **safety & certification (CE/UL/ISO 10218/13849)**, **IP**.
- Demote: render-only product visuals, generic "robots will take over" TAM.

## 5. Proof devices
Unedited field video (uncut, one take); fleet uptime dashboard; pilot-to-PO table; teardown cost chart; manufacturing partner letter; certification milestones; factory photos.

## 6. Design and tone
Hardware-forward: real product photography/video, exploded views, cost charts. Engineering honesty (show failures and fixes); avoid CGI-only. Concise technical captions.

## 7. Red flags
Teleoperated demos presented as autonomy; single-source Shenzhen supplier; BOM that does not scale; no pilot conversion; capex underestimated; reliance on one customer; foundation-model claims without data scale; tariffs ignored.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Physical AI/humanoid funding exploding: >$5B in four months of 2026 > all 2025 ($3.2B) ([KeyBanc/PitchBook](https://www.key.com/businesses-institutions/business-expertise/articles/humanoid-robots.html)).
- Europe: strong research and industrial customers but less scale capital than US, less manufacturing momentum than China ([AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/robotics/neura-robotics-raises-1-4b-physical-ai) context).
- Strategic chip/industrial investors fill large rounds.
