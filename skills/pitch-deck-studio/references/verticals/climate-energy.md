## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Demand pull today: who signs an offtake/PPA; "being green is no longer a sufficient pitch": hard savings or revenue ([Breega 2026](https://www.breega.com/insights/where-is-european-climatetech-infrastructure-investment-heading-in-2026)).
2. Cost curve: LCOE/$ per kWh/$ per tonne vs incumbents without subsidy; learning rate.
3. Technology and scale risk: TRL, pilot-to-FOAK-to-NOAK path, capex per unit.
4. Financing structure: how FOAK gets funded (equity vs project debt vs grants); bankability of offtakes (counterparty, durability, stress-tested).
5. Policy exposure (tax credits, tariffs, DOE support), permitting/interconnection.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 14-18.
- Promote: **unit economics / cost curve vs incumbent**, **offtake & customer pipeline**, **tech de-risking milestones (TRL ladder)**, **financing roadmap (equity -> grants -> project debt)**.
- Add: **policy sensitivity**, **permitting/interconnection plan**, **LCA/carbon accounting method**, **IP**.
- Demote: climate-impact generalities; ESG language; long TAM in gigatons.

## 5. Proof devices
Pilot data and third-party validation (national labs, independent engineer); signed LOI/PPA/offtake with named counterparty; cost-curve chart with sensitivity; site photos/video; DOE/EIC/ARPA-E awards; LCA by recognized method.

## 6. Design and tone
Engineering-credible and commercial: process diagrams, cost charts, photos of real hardware; avoid greenwashing imagery. Frame as "cheaper/more reliable/faster to power", not virtue.

## 7. Red flags
Technology without a buyer; cost parity relying on subsidy; FOAK capex unfunded; offtakes non-binding with weak counterparties; claims on carbon accounting without method; policy cliff exposure; long permitting with no plan.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- H1 2026 climate VC $26.1B, +55% YoY, driven by data-center power demand; record cleantech IPO/M&A quarter (Fervo, X-Energy) ([CTVC](https://www.ctvc.co/h126-climate-tech-funding-up-55-to-26bn-thanks-to-data-centers/), [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/climate-tech-funding-surges-in-h1-2026-on-growing-data-center-demand-for-low-carbon-power-report/)).
- Tracker definitions diverge widely ($13.2B to $41.3B) so cite the source.
- Institutional investors remain wary without offtakes/proven results ([Carbon Pulse](https://carbon-pulse.com/511563/)); firm power (geothermal, nuclear, fusion) and grid/data-center themes lead.
