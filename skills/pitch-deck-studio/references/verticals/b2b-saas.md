# B2B SaaS

Legend: linked = 2026 sweep; unlinked names = background, verify; **[pre-2024]** = older benchmark.

## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: SaaS-specialist seed/A funds (Point Nine, Craft, Heavybit for dev, Unusual, Wing, Costanoa); multi-stage (Bessemer, Accel, Index, Redpoint, Insight, Battery, Emergence, Foundation, ICONIQ at growth); YC and angels; AI-first platforms.
- Evidence: Accel, a16z, Benchmark, Sequoia named as very active seed investors ([Causo US seed list, 2026](https://hub.causo.ai/lists/seed-fintech-vcs-us-2026), not SaaS-specific); General Catalyst, Lightspeed, Index feature in recent counts ([Crunchbase Q2 2026](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/fintech-funder-general-catalyst-leads-deal-count-q2-2026/)). Verify specialist funds in the sweep.

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Growth vs efficiency: ARR trajectory with burn multiple; "efficiency now ranks above raw growth" ([Causo H1 2026 B2B SaaS GTM report](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/h1-2026-b2b-saas-gtm-report), vendor).
2. Retention: GRR/NRR by cohort and segment.
3. GTM repeatability: ICP, sales cycle, ACV, rep productivity, pipeline coverage; founder-led sales to repeatable motion.
4. AI disruption: is the seat-based product exposed to agents; pricing shift.
5. Customer concentration and expansion path.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 12-15 + appendix at A; 10-12 at seed.
- Promote: ARR + cohort retention (slide 3-5), ICP and sales motion, pricing/packaging, unit economics (CAC payback, magic number), pipeline coverage, product screenshots.
- Add: **AI-defensibility/pricing-shift slide** (seats vs usage/outcome), **customer logos with expansion**, hiring plan to Series B milestones.
- Demote: competitor feature matrices, long vision, TAM top-down.

## 5. Proof devices
ARR waterfall chart (new/expansion/churn); cohort retention heatmap; named customer quotes with ROI; sales-cycle days; logo wall segmented by ACV; payback chart; case study with before/after metric.

## 6. Design and tone
Clean, data-forward, product UI screens, charts with real axes. Analytical, understated, no hype adjectives. Consistent metric definitions (state ARR vs run-rate vs contracted).

## 7. Red flags
Services revenue labelled ARR; one customer >20-30% of ARR; NRR <100% by cohort; payback >24 months; sales cycles lengthening; pricing based on seats in an agent-disrupted workflow; unexplained ARR definitions.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Series A median software round $14.4M at $80M post (Carta Jul 2026) up from $7.4M at $48M pre a year earlier, a mix effect largely AI ([Waveup](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/)).
- Private SaaS valuations 3-7x ARR vs AI-native 25-30x ([Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/05/10/saas-metrics-founders-must-track-2026-ltv-cac-nrr-churn-cac-payback-benchmarks-guide), vendor, unverified).
- Efficiency over growth-at-all-costs; ARR bar rising ($2-3M, sometimes $4M, per Uncork May 2026).
