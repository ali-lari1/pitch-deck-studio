## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Real usage and revenue vs token speculation: fees, TVL quality, retained users, not incentive-driven volume.
2. Regulatory posture: licensed stablecoin/payments structure; securities-law view on token; jurisdiction.
3. Token design and treasury (if any): supply schedule, unlocks, equity-vs-token rights, governance; why a token is needed.
4. Security: audits, exploits history, key management, custody.
5. Defensibility vs incumbents (Stripe, Circle, Coinbase) and distribution.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 12-15.
- Promote: regulatory/legal structure, institutional/real-world use case, traction excluding incentives, security.
- Add: **tokenomics & rights slide** (equity vs token vs warrant), **compliance map by jurisdiction**, **audit/security slide**, **stablecoin/partner stack**.
- Demote: price charts, community-size vanity metrics, "web3 will change everything".

## 5. Proof devices
On-chain dashboards (Dune/Artemis links) showing organic users; audited contracts and bug-bounty history; licensed partner logos; regulated pilot volumes; wallet/agent demo.

## 6. Design and tone
Sober-institutional for stablecoin/infra (investors call this "regulated financial plumbing"); crypto-native cultural tone is penalized at institutional LPs. Precise on legal, no moon language.

## 7. Red flags
Incentive-farmed metrics; unlimited token supply with insider unlock cliffs; no licence plan; prior hacks; founders anonymous without track record; token as fundraising shortcut; dependence on single stablecoin issuer.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Capital is concentrated in trading infrastructure, stablecoin payments, regulated finance, tokenization, custody, compliance, data ([AlphaMaven/Cryptorank](https://alpha-maven.com/story/crypto/crypto-fundraising-recap-q3-2026-cryptorank)).
- Crypto VC "slump" framing in May 2026 even as large funds still close (Haun $1B).
- TradFi capital entering via strategic checks (Citadel Securities $400M into Crypto.com).
