## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: real-estate specialists (Fifth Wall, MetaProp, Brick & Mortar Ventures, Camber Creek, RET Ventures, Navitas, Moderne Ventures, Bessemer-style vertical SaaS investors); construction/industrial (Building Ventures, Trucks VC, Cemex Ventures, Procore Ventures); real-estate owner/operator CVCs (Brookfield, Prologis, CBRE, JLL Spark); generalists for AI back-office; YC (more than a dozen real-estate/construction startups in Summer 2026).
- Evidence: Brick & Mortar Ventures (founded 2015, $100M Fund I in 2019, largest PlanGrid seed investor; Commercial Observer Oct 2026) ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/power-series/player/darren-bechtel/)); Xpanner $18M Series B (construction robotics) ([MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/engineering-and-construction/ai-drives-a-wave-of-proptech-funding-as-construction-and-commercial-real-estate-startups-attract-fresh-capital)). Fifth Wall and MetaProp 2026 deals not found in sweep: verify.

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Adoption friction: sales cycle with owners/GCs/subs, integration with Procore/Yardi/MRI/Autodesk.
2. Measurable ROI: schedule days saved, change-order reduction, NOI uplift, labor hours.
3. Is it software or tech-enabled services/hardware (margin and scaling)?
4. Cyclicality: interest-rate sensitivity, transaction volume exposure.
5. Distribution through incumbents; pricing per project vs per seat; pilot-to-roll-out conversion.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 11-14.
- Promote: **ROI case study with numbers**, **customer pipeline and integration**, **sales cycle & pricing**.
- Add: **channel/incumbent partnership**, **cyclicality stress test**, **field adoption proof (users on site)**.
- Demote: broad "$X trillion industry" TAM; vision renders.

## 5. Proof devices
Project-level before/after (days, $), site photos with product in use, GC/owner quotes, integration logos, unit-level pricing economics, usage by trade partners.

## 6. Design and tone
Practical and visual: photos of real sites and buildings, maps, floor-plan visuals; industry vocabulary; conservative palette; credibility with operators.

## 7. Red flags
Long unpaid pilots; reliance on one GC/owner; low field adoption; rate-cycle exposure; services margins sold as software; no integration with systems of record; hardware without deployment plan.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- AI central to nearly every construction/real-estate deal (Crunchbase News via MarketScale).
- YC S26 heavy on real-estate/construction back-office AI.
- Construction robotics/physical AI rounds appear (Xpanner $18M B).
