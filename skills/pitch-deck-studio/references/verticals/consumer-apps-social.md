# Consumer apps / social

Legend: linked = 2026 sweep; unlinked = background, verify; **[pre-2024]** = older benchmark.

## 1. Who funds it now
- Archetypes: consumer specialists (Forerunner, Spark, NFX, Kindred, Lightspeed consumer, a16z consumer/Apps Fund, Index, Accel, Thrive); YC and pre-seed funds; AI-consumer crossover (Nvidia, NEA, Salesforce Ventures); creators/angels (founders of consumer apps).
- Evidence: Forerunner Fund VII $500M (Nov 2024, ~$3B AUM) with thesis "Human Insight in the Age of AI"; a16z $1.7B Apps Fund aimed at consumer AI; repeat investors in consumer AI: a16z, Accel, Nvidia, NEA, Lightspeed, Spark, NFDG, Salesforce Ventures ([newmarketpitch tracker Aug 2024-Jul 2026](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/consumer-ai-funding-analysis), [Peony consumer investors](https://peony.ink/blog/consumer-investors); vendor blogs). Examples: Rosebud $6M seed, Pallie AI $2M, Patronus EUR 11M led by 3TS (Apr 2026), Tolan $30M (Oct 2026, single source).

## 2. What they scrutinize first
1. Retention cohorts (D1/D7/D30/D90 or W-retention curves flattening) before growth.
2. Organic vs paid growth; viral coefficient; CAC vs LTV; channel durability.
3. Engagement depth (DAU/MAU, sessions, time) and monetization path (subscription, ads, commerce).
4. Founder insight and taste; why this behavior is durable (not novelty), and platform risk (Apple/Google/OpenAI).
5. For AI consumer: gross margin after inference, safety for minors/companions.

## 4. Slide emphasis
- Ideal: 10-12.
- Promote: **product screens/video (slide 2)**, **retention cohort chart**, **growth channel mix (organic %)**, **founder insight/taste**.
- Add: **brand & community**, **monetization plan with tests**, **trust & safety (esp. minors)**, **platform dependency plan**.
- Demote: TAM by population; financial model beyond 18 months; competitor logo grid.

## 5. Proof devices
Cohort retention heatmap; organic share pie; waitlist-to-activation funnel; screen recordings of real user sessions; user quotes/screenshots of organic virality; app-store ratings and rank; weekly growth line.

## 6. Design and tone
Brand-forward and visual: product UI as hero, big imagery, short copy, personality and taste signal (consumer investors fund founder taste). Warm, human, confident. Numbers still required but styled.

## 7. Red flags
Paid-only growth with decaying cohorts; novelty spike; no retention plateau; dependence on one platform algorithm or API; AI companion safety/regulatory risk; monetization never tested; founder lacks consumer insight.

## 8. 2025-26 context
- Consumer venture funding is bifurcated: AI-native consumer apps get capital; classic consumer/DTC starved (one blog says DTC funding fell 97% from peak; verify) ([Peony](https://peony.ink/blog/consumer-investors)).
- Subscription growth concentrates at the top; median app barely grows (RevenueCat 2026).
- Large consumer-AI rounds from a16z/Accel/Nvidia dominate the tracker (newmarketpitch).
